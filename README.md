# IRRBB
IRRBB repository comprises  financial and mathematical foundations of Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book (IRRBB) modeling, including Net Interest Income (NII), Economic Value of Equity (EVE), and Funds Transfer Pricing (FTP).
Session Structure
1. Financial & Mathematical Prerequisites for IRRBB Modeling (1 Hour)
Key Topics:

Fixed Income Mathematics:

Time value of money (TVM)

Yield curves, bootstrapping, and interpolation

Discount factors, zero-coupon bonds, and forward rates

Interest Rate Risk Concepts:

Duration (Macaulay, Modified, Effective)

Convexity and basis risk

Gap analysis (repricing mismatches)

Statistical Methods:

Monte Carlo simulations for rate shocks

Regression analysis for behavioral modeling (non-maturity deposits)

Hands-on Exercise:

Calculate duration and convexity of a bond portfolio using Python/Excel.

Construct a yield curve using bootstrapping.

2. IRRBB Regulatory Framework & Key Metrics (1.5 Hours)
Key Topics:

Basel III/IV IRRBB Standards

Earnings-based approach (NII at Risk)

Economic value-based approach (EVE at Risk)

Key IRRBB Measures:

Net Interest Income (NII) Sensitivity

Parallel & non-parallel rate shocks

Dynamic vs. static balance sheet assumptions

Economic Value of Equity (EVE) Sensitivity

Discounted cash flow (DCF) approach

Impact of optionality (prepayments, caps/floors)

Hands-on Exercise:

Simulate NII impact under +100bps and -100bps rate shocks.

Compute EVE using a simplified banking book (loans & deposits).

3. Fund Transfer Pricing (FTP) & IRRBB Linkages (1 Hour)
Key Topics:

Role of FTP in IRRBB management

Constructing a bank’s funding curve

Carry cost analysis & Treasury PnL impact

Behavioral adjustments (non-maturity deposits, prepayments)

Hands-on Exercise:

Derive an internal FTP curve and assess its impact on business unit profitability.

4. Quantitative IRRBB Modeling in Practice (2 Hours)
Key Topics:

Modeling Approaches:

Static vs. dynamic balance sheet modeling

Behavioral assumptions (deposit betas, loan prepayments)

Regulatory Reporting (EBA/FRB/BCBS Standards)

Stress testing scenarios (rate ramp, instantaneous shocks)

Sensitivity analysis and hedging strategies

Hands-on Exercise:

Build a simplified NII-at-Risk model in Python/Excel.

Compute EVE under different yield curve scenarios.

5. Case Study: IRRBB in a Large Wall Street Bank (1.5 Hours)
Scenario:

A bank has a large portfolio of fixed-rate mortgages and non-maturity deposits.

Assess IRRBB exposure under rising/falling rate environments.

Recommend hedging strategies (swaps, options, balance sheet restructuring).

Hands-on Exercise:

Simulate hedging effectiveness using interest rate swaps.

Compute regulatory capital impact under stressed scenarios.

Tools & Data Required
Software: Python (NumPy, Pandas), Excel/VBA, Bloomberg/Refinitiv for market data

Data: Sample banking book (loans, deposits, securities), yield curve data

Who Should Attend?
Risk managers, ALM (Asset-Liability Management) professionals

Treasury and FTP analysts

Quantitative modelers in banking/finance

Regulatory reporting specialists

Outcome
By the end of the session, participants will:
✔ Understand core IRRBB concepts (NII, EVE, FTP) and their mathematical foundations.
✔ Be able to construct basic IRRBB models for sensitivity analysis.
✔ Gain insights into regulatory reporting requirements.
✔ Apply learnings to real-world banking book risk management.
